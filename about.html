Our Story

Every wish deserves to feel personal

PreCardAI started with a simple idea: the cards people send for birthdays, weddings, and festivals should feel like they were made just for the person receiving them — not picked off a shelf.

We built a place where anyone can turn a photo and a feeling into a wish worth sending — no design skills, no expensive software, just a template, your own words (or AI to help find them), and a moment made a little more special.

What we believe

Wishes, not invitations

PreCardAI cards are for sending love, not managing guest lists — simple, warm, and personal.

Made for every occasion

Birthdays, weddings, pooja, Diwali, and more — because every celebration deserves its own card.

AI that helps, not replaces

Our AI suggests words when you need them — the feeling behind the wish is always yours.

Free to start

Anyone can create and download a card for free — no signup required to try it out.

Where we're headed

We're adding more occasions, more designs, and new ways to make a wish feel personal — including voice messages and reminders so you never miss a moment that matters.
